Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are and how we use them. You should read this policy so you can understand what type of cookies we use, or the information we collect using cookies and how that information is used.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how people use the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

VEXOR PDF Generator is a static, local-first tool. We do not use any tracking cookies or third-party analytical cookies. You can use our PDF generation tools knowing that your activity is not being monitored or tracked by us.

3. Local Storage

We may occasionally use local storage (a browser feature similar to cookies) to save your generation settings (like margin sizes or page formats) so you don't have to re-enter them on your next visit. This data never leaves your device.
